# Design brief — Macros and Mamas marketing landing (for Claude Artifact)

Use this if you want Claude to rebuild/edit an interactive artifact. A Cursor HTML mockup already exists at `marketing-landing-mockup-v1.html`.

## Brand

- Background: `#FAF5F2`
- Ink: `#33272E` / soft `#6E5D66`
- Accent: `#B4416B` / deep `#8E2F53` / soft `#F6E4EC`
- Sage: `#5F8168` / soft `#E6EFE8`
- Fonts: **Marcellus** (display/brand), **Karla** (UI/body)
- Avoid: purple gradients, Inter/Roboto, cream+#terracotta cliché, dark-mode default, pill-stat clutter in the hero

## Hero rules (hard)

- Full-bleed photo background (Callie hero): edge-to-edge, not an inset card
- Brand name **Macros and Mamas** is the dominant text signal
- One headline, one short lede, one primary CTA + secondary sign-in
- No stats, schedules, or floating badges on the hero image

## Sections (in order)

1. Hero — waitlist CTA, “Lock in $249 — $50 off $299”
2. Horizontal phone frames (4): Today+Snap · Menu picks · Week plan+grocery · Messages+push  
   Label as placeholders until real screenshots exist
3. What’s inside — 6 concrete capabilities (human macros, snap/menu AI, plan+grocery, water/habits/weigh-ins, Messages+WhatsApp+Monday voice, home-screen PWA)
4. How it works — 4 steps: waitlist → pay when open → intake → Callie unlocks
5. Meet Callie — photo + short trust copy
6. FAQ — who for, price, not just WhatsApp, no gym required
7. Final waitlist CTA

## Offer copy

- Enrollment closed; cohort 2 waitlist
- Early price $249 vs full $299
- Primary CTA: Join the waitlist
- Secondary: Already enrolled? Sign in

## Output

Single mobile-first HTML page, max-width ~560px content column, sticky note at top that it’s a mockup.
