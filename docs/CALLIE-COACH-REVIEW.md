## How the coach decides (plain English)

A mama types something. The app tries three doors, in this order. The first one that fits wins. The others never run.

| Door | What it is | Cost | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Your sentence** | A question you already answered, in your words. Instant. | Free | “Should I skip dinner?” → *You never skip a meal!* |
| **2. The phone** | Her ranges, today’s log, your recipe bank, My meals. Math, not a guess. | Free | “What should I eat?” → three cards that fit |
| **3. The model** | A real question the first two cannot answer. It reads the sentence and (if she sent one) the photo. | Counts toward 30 / day | “What should I get at In-N-Out?” |

Then a fourth door, which is you:

| Door | What it is | What she sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **4. You** | Supply, guilt, the scale, changing her ranges, billing, anything clinical. The bot does not answer. | “Message Callie” — it does **not** send the message for her. It opens her 1:1 thread with a draft. She still taps Send. |

**The model is never the source of truth for a number.** If it invents macros that do not add up, the card is dropped.

## We are not pasting your raw messages in

This is the important part.

When you text Patrick “for Italian I would do protein and a side, pasta is the side not the main, something something Oreo is not real food…” that is a **note**. It is not a finished product sentence, and it is not a rule for when to say it.

If we drop a note in raw, two bad things happen:

- The bot says too much (Italian + Oreos in one breath).
- The bot says it at the wrong time (`what can I eat` looked like `is pizza ok`).

**What we actually do:**

1. You give us **one job** (Italian, coffee, skip a meal, Chipotle…).
2. You give us **when to say it** (example questions, and when *not* to say it).
3. You give us **one or two short sentences** — the thing she should read.
4. We lock that sentence. The bot says exactly that. We do not let a model rewrite it.

A model only speaks when there is no locked sentence — a named restaurant we have not scripted, a fridge photo, “I have chicken and rice and I need 40g protein.”

So: keep writing the way you talk. Then, for anything you want in the product, put it in the template at the bottom. We will encode that. A Slack paragraph is research, not a ship.

## Your locked sentences (review these word for word)

These are live. Rewrite any line you do not like. If you rewrite it, we ship your words.

### Eating out, no restaurant named

**When:** “I’m eating out”, “I’m at a restaurant, no nutrition info”, “what should I order” — and she did **not** name a place or a cuisine.

> Use the PS method: protein and a side. Roasted chicken, a burger, steak — then rice, salad or potatoes. Eating out makes it really hard not to blow through fat, so do your best. Dressing on the side, and dip your fork as you go.

### Italian (no specific restaurant)

**When:** she says Italian. **Not** when she says In-N-Out, Chipotle, etc.

> For Italian, protein and a side: roasted chicken or fish, and pasta with tomato sauce as the side — not the main. Pasta is low protein, so it sits next to the protein rather than filling the plate.

### Never skip a meal

**When:** skip dinner / skip lunch / “I’m over, should I even eat?”

> Absolutely not. You never skip a meal! The goal isn't to nail your macros every single time — it's to nourish yourself and learn how to fuel your body. Eat something simple and lower calorie: grilled chicken and rice, or even a protein shake. Follow your hunger, too. Some days you burned more, and those days need more.

### “Is this ok?” / pizza / protein bar / Oreo

**When:** “is pizza ok”, “can I have a protein bar”, “what about a slice”. **Not** “what can I eat for Italian.”

> Any real food can fit your macros. A slice of pizza is real food — water, yeast, flour, tomatoes, cheese. An Oreo is not; it's full of stuff made in a lab. We can make macros work for real food. If this one blows through fat, calories or carbs, next time log it ahead and keep breakfast and lunch lower fat or lower carb so it fits.

### Alcohol

**When:** alcohol, wine, cocktail, beer, tequila, “cheers”, “drinks”.

> Alcohol is up to you. We don't encourage it and we don't say absolutely not. If you have a drink, keep fat in range — that's the one that adds up — and drink it with food, not on an empty stomach.

### Coffee

**When:** coffee, caffeine, espresso, latte, cold brew.

> Coffee is allowed — Callie has a cup or two a day — but never on an empty stomach. Have it with breakfast. And after about 7 hours, half the caffeine is still in your blood, so if falling asleep is hard, it may be the afternoon cup.

### Intermittent fasting

**When:** intermittent fasting, 16:8, OMAD, “should I start fasting?”

> No intermittent fasting. When we skip meals the body leans on cortisol, a stress hormone, to stay energized, and that pulls from the same nutrients your sex hormones need. We eat consistently and we fill up at our meals.

### Sweeteners / Diet Coke

**When:** aspartame, sucralose, stevia, Splenda, Diet Coke, Diet Pepsi, zero sugar soda.

> I'd encourage skipping artificial sweeteners — they're not something we want daily. If a Diet Coke is a real thing for you, try an Olipop vintage cola instead. One swap, no lecture.

### Under on the day, protein already hit

**When:** “I hit my protein but have calories left”, “should I eat more or stop?”, “done for the day”.

> If you've eaten three square meals, you can leave the rest. Follow your hunger. We never want to eat less than 50g of fat in a day — hormones really do need it.

Plus, only if it’s true that day:

> You're under 50g of fat so far, so get some in if you can.

> Have you eaten your carbs too?

### She skipped a meal the clock already passed

The bot notices. It does not silently spend that room on lunch.

> I noticed you skipped a meal. We really want to eat consistently for hormonal health. When we skip, the body can use cortisol — a stress hormone — to keep going, and that takes the same nutrients from our sex hormones. Eat a real lunch, a larger snack, and a larger dinner so you still reach your goals.

If breakfast was the one:

> Mornings can be busy, and a small appetite is often a blunted metabolism talking. Try starting with a protein shake if a full breakfast isn't easy yet.
