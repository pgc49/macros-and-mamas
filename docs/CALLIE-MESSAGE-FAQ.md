## Her voice in these texts

This is texting Callie, not the Meal Coach. A lot of it should stay in Messages.

What she does constantly:

- She opens warm and short. "Hey love" in 95 replies, "hey boo" in 45, "dude" in 102, a hey-something start in 163. "Mama" is one of her most used words (174).
- She is emphatic. 1,027 of 1,423 replies have an exclamation point. "Lol" shows up 81 times, "crushing" 45, "yay" 25. Emoji almost never (2 messages).
- She invites instead of ruling. "Let's" is in 80 replies. Food advice sounds like "could we try this" and then a real food: a shake first on a busy morning, cottage cheese with fruit, a wrap or a sandwich with protein on it, carbs and protein earlier in the day.
- She asks back. Energy, appetite, and "how are you feeling" show up in 54 replies. "Love" is her top word (258), then protein (191) and macros (181).
- The foods she actually names are shake, chicken, fruit, yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, breakfast. Not a method slogan.
- She reassures, then she adjusts. Praise and "you're doing great" sit in front of the correction.
- About 21 replies point at a voice note she recorded. Mamas answer those. The coach does not have that channel.

What she does in texts that the coach must not copy:

- The scale. She mentions weight or the scale in 52 replies, including what someone is aiming for and whether the loss is too fast. That stays her conversation. The coach already refuses it.
- Supply, in the specific way she talks to one mama about milk. A passing mention is already the protected-supply preface. A supply problem stays a handoff.
- Pet names as a habit ("hey love", "hey boo", "dude") and "good girl" (5 replies). That is a relationship. It would be strange from a screen.
- Stretched-out words, "lol", and typos. That is her texting. The coach stays plain on purpose.
- Her own family, and anything a mama told her about hers.

What did **not** show up in her DMs, even though it is locked in the coach from the questionnaire: "PS method", "protein and a side", "dressing on the side", "real food", "you never skip". Those lines are still hers from the review. They are just not how she texts. "Cheat" showed up once. "Simply" five times. She is not lecturing with those words.

## What this means for the coach

The model is not trained on these messages, and it still cannot see a mama's thread. This pass is a brief for Callie, not a fine-tune.

The coach's job (pick the next meal) is a question they ask the app, not a question they text her. What they text her is "how am I doing", the scale, hunger, supply, a specific plate, and life. Borrowing her voice means borrowing the short, warm, food-specific, ask-a-question-back part. It does not mean teaching the coach to talk about weight, milk, or to greet someone like a friend who has their number.
