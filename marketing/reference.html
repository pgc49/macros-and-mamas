The philosophy

You don't need one number. You need room.

Calculators hand you a single rigid target and call it a plan. Then Tuesday happens. Callie builds you a band instead: calories, protein, carbs, and fat as ranges. Busy, active day? Eat the top. Slow day, sick kid, no sleep? The bottom. Both count as a win.

Your ranges flex with nursing, activity, stress, and how fast you're actually losing. This is what a plan looks like when it's built for a mother, not a spreadsheet.

Questions mamas actually ask

Before you ask

Is this a crash diet?

The opposite. Your ranges are built to protect muscle and, if you're nursing, milk supply. Lose faster than your plan calls for and the app flags it, then Callie raises your food. We are firmly against 1,200-calorie culture.

Can I do this while breastfeeding?

Yes, if you're at least 3 months postpartum. Callie coaches supply-aware and your ranges account for nursing from day one. Under 3 months? Join the waitlist now and start when your supply is established.

How much of Callie do I actually get?

Callie personally reads your intake and builds your ranges. She's in the group coaching thread Monday through Friday, usually in voice notes, and her answers teach the whole group. You also get 1:1 Messages and the Monday voice note every week. This is not a bot with her name on it.

Are there hidden costs? Shakes? Supplements?

No. One price covers the full 8 weeks, the app, and the coaching. The food is real food from your regular grocery store, built around what your family already eats.

Do I need a gym?

No. The daily rhythm works with walks, home strength, and whatever your season allows. Your ranges are built around your real activity, not an aspirational one.

Will I have to track food forever?

No, and that's the point. Eight weeks of honest logging builds plate intuition: you learn what your portions look like so you can eyeball a plate and get on with your life.

What happens when I have a bad week?

You will, and it's built in. Log it (you can log past days), tell Callie what happened, reset Monday. No shame spiral, no starting over from zero. The pattern over weeks matters more than any single day.

Do I have to be a mom, or postpartum?

No. The coaching is postpartum-aware because that's where the worst advice lives, but any woman who wants ranges, real food, and a human coach is welcome.

Is this an App Store app?

It's a web app you add to your home screen from the link we send. Same icon, same push notifications, nothing to download or update. About 20 seconds to set up, and we walk you through it.
